Terms & Conditions of service

Last updated 25 September 2026. These terms cover bookings made with Iron & Oak Barber Co., 24 Market Street, Maboneng, Johannesburg.

The booking

A booking holds one chair for one hour, from the hour you choose until the next hour. Monday to Friday the book runs 08:00–19:00 (last chair 18:00–19:00). Saturday it runs 08:00–17:00 (last chair 16:00–17:00). We are closed on Sunday. You can book up to 90 days ahead.

Each hour can take 8 spots. Once 8 confirmed bookings share that hour, it shows as full and will not accept another guest. A spot is yours only after you receive a receipt with a booking ID.

Your receipt and booking ID

The receipt shows your name, service, price, barber, day, hour and booking ID (it looks like IO-ABC123). Keep the ID. Anyone with it can look the visit up or cancel it, so treat it like a ticket. You can add the same appointment to Google Calendar, or download an Apple and Outlook calendar file. The calendar event uses the day and hour you chose and lasts one hour.

Prices

Prices are in South African rand and are confirmed on your receipt: Classic Haircut R220, Skin Fade R260, Beard Trim & Line-up R150, Cut & Beard Package R340, Kids Cut (under 12) R140, Hot Towel Shave R190. You pay at the shop. We do not take payment on this website. Prices can change for future bookings; a confirmed receipt keeps the price shown on it.

Cancellation and changes

Cancel or move a booking from this website with your booking ID. Cancelling frees the spot straight away. Please cancel or move at least 4 hours before the chair starts. Later cancellations and no-shows may be charged 50% of the service price on your next visit. We do not charge that fee through the website.

Arrival

Please arrive 5 minutes early. If you are more than 15 minutes late we may have to give the chair away and ask you to rebook. Tell your barber about skin sensitivities before a shave or chemical product is used.

Children, offers and house rules

  • Kids cuts are for children under 12 and a parent or guardian stays in the shop.
  • Code FIRSTCHAIR takes 15% off a guest’s first cut, mentioned at the till. One use per guest. It is not a cash discount on products and we may end the offer.
  • Gift vouchers, when sold, are valid for 12 months and are not refunded for cash.
  • We can refuse service that puts staff or other guests at risk.

Liability and the law

We take care with every cut. We are not liable for a reaction you did not tell us about, or for indirect loss.
