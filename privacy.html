Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026. Iron & Oak Barber Co. is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website, in terms of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

When you book, we collect your name, email address, phone number, chosen service, barber, day, hour and booking ID. We also store the time the booking was made and whether it is confirmed or cancelled.

Why we collect it

  • To hold your chair and show how many of the 8 spots in that hour are left.
  • To give you a receipt and let you track, move or cancel with the booking ID.
  • To contact you about that appointment if we have to.

The legal basis is the booking you asked us to make. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Where it is stored

Bookings are stored in a Google Sheet named BARBER-ONE, operated by the shop through Google. Google may store that sheet outside South Africa. Booking IDs you have used on a device are also saved in that browser so My Bookings can find them. See the cookie policy.

Your booking ID is the key to that record. Anyone who has the ID can view or cancel that booking. Keep the receipt private.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for 24 months after the appointment so we can resolve a complaint or a late-cancel query, then we delete or de-identify them. You can ask us to delete a record sooner if we do not still need it for a legal claim.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your booking details, or object to how we use them. Email chair@ironoakbarber.co.za with your booking ID. We will respond within a reasonable time. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

A parent or guardian books a kids cut and gives their own contact details. We do not ask a child to create an account.
