Built on Market Street, one chair at a time.

Iron & Oak opened in 2015 with two chairs and a single clipper. A decade on, we're a five-barber shop known across Maboneng for consistent fades and a shop floor that still smells like bay rum. We train on the tools, not just the trends — every barber here served an apprenticeship before touching a paying client.
