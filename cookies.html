Cookie policy

Last updated 25 September 2026. This page explains what Iron & Oak stores on your device when you use this website.

What we store

We do not run advertising cookies, analytics cookies or social trackers. We use browser storage for two essential things:

  • Cookie choice. We remember whether you accepted or limited storage to essential items, so the notice does not keep returning.
  • Booking IDs. After you book, or after you look an ID up, we save that ID on this device so My Bookings can track or cancel the visit. The booking itself lives in our Google Sheet.
  • Offer notice. We remember for this browser session that you closed the first-visit offer, so it does not pop up again while you browse.

Your choice

Accept and Essential only both allow the items above, because the site does not set anything else. Essential only is there so the choice is explicit. You can clear these in your browser settings (site data / local storage for this website). Clearing them removes the IDs from this device; it does not cancel a booking. Cancel with the booking ID on the booking page.

Google Calendar opens on Google’s site only if you choose Add to Google Calendar. That visit is covered by Google’s own policies, not by a cookie we set.
